Hey there, I'm Abigail

I lived in 6 cities and visited 14 countries before the age of ten.

I should have known that the little girl who kept a travel journal (yes the type with ticket stubs, boarding passes and notes from each day...) would end up a travel-obsessed photographer, blogger and podcaster.

Now I share stories, travel tips and create itineraries for other adventure-loving travellers just like you.

Long story long...

If you love adventure, road trips and the outdoors, you're in the right place

I'm one for slower travel, and I'll bet you are too

I love pretty things, I love the outdoors, I love experiencing things which make me feel alive. I am happiest when we're on a road trip, with nowhere to be and nothing to do. I love to sit with the van door open watching the waves crash on the sand. I love to have dinner beside the mountains, as the moon lights up the sky.

I love falling asleep to the sound of rain as it falls on the van roof. I love waking up to the golden sunlight on my face.

I walk, I swim, I sit, I see, I smell, I listen, I breathe.
Because it makes me feel alive.

Because life is worth living.

Let me be part of your journey

I want you to experience Aotearoa the way I do

I don't care about ticking off countries like a to-do list, I believe that adventure can be had wherever you live.

We find ourselves returning to the same places in Aotearoa over and over again, and this means I have only the best advice for your trip.
