Long story long

From spreadsheets to a green van

I moved back to Auckland at 18 after growing up in India, and it was a rough landing. A couple of years at uni were a real struggle with my mental health, and the way back was not a straight line. Alongside a lot of therapy, what helped most was time outside. A camera gave me a reason to go and find the light. A podcast, a few years later, gave me a reason to keep going back out and asking people about their adventures. Both still do.

Three years ago I was a full-time urban planner. Two-hundred-page reports, council meetings, the rush-hour commute. I sent my first brand pitch at 1,200 followers, phoned a friend the whole time because I had no idea what to say, and slammed the laptop shut the second I hit send. They said yes. That one yes opened my eyes to the possibilities of the creator world.

Then came a Thursday sunrise on a snowy South Island mountain, kea circling, and one thought: I need to be on more mountains on more Thursday mornings. I quit the following Tuesday with nothing close to a safety net, just enough delulu confidence to back myself.

Since then we've built our dream van, paddled 500km down the East Coast from Cape Rēinga on the Slow Paddle with my husband Isaac, walked 85km solo on the Old Ghost Road, and put out a new episode every Friday for four years.

You don't have to stay stuck on the path you're on. That's the whole point of this website.

If you love adventure, road trips and the outdoors, you're in the right place

I'm one for slower travel, and I'll bet you are too

I love pretty things, I love the outdoors, I love experiencing things which make me feel alive. I am happiest when we're on a road trip, with nowhere to be and nothing to do. I love to sit with the van door open watching the waves crash on the sand. I love to have dinner beside the mountains, as the moon lights up the sky.

I love falling asleep to the sound of rain as it falls on the van roof. I love waking up to the golden sunlight on my face.

I walk, I swim, I sit, I see, I smell, I listen, I breathe.
Because it makes me feel alive.

Because life is worth living.
