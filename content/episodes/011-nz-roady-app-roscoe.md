When the pandemic disrupted Roscoe's travel company, he pivoted the business and created the Roady App. Pitching to investors at a shark-tank like situation with just an idea, Roscoe got the funding he needed to develop the app. Roscoe shares how he got to explore New Zealand and work with tourism boards, creating incredible content and ticking off bucketlist activities. The Roady App launched in 2022 with 400+ experiences to tick off. The best part is getting to compete with friends on the leader board and level up your travel game through earning all the badges.
