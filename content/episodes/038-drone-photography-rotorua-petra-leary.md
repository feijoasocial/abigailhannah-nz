Hooked from her first flight, drone photographer Petra Leary shares her love for the art form. Her breathtaking compositions capture the symmetry and patterns of natural and urban landscapes. She talks about a recent trip to Rotorua, where she captured the unique geothermal textures and colours of mudpools and steamy lakes. Petra also shares advice for aspiring drone photographers and details the different rules, licenses and permits needed to fly in New Zealand.
