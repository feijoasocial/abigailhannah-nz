She visited over 60 countries before she visited the South Island, but now Tegan is excited to call Aotearoa home and makes the most travelling on the weekends, with her cat Gatsby Le Chat and her dog Senna in tow. A Northland girl, Tegan shares all her best advice for anyone that wants to travel with their pets - the questions to ask your accommodation host, what to pack, pet-friendly activity ideas, the best region to start (spoiler alert, it's Taupō) - she shares it all. This episode is packed with tips, tricks and inspiration to encourage you to take your furry friends on your next holiday.
