Your host Abigail Hannah shares all the details on her most recent Great Walk adventure - a last minute trip to hike the Milford Track. She details what to expect on each day of the track and valuable advice for the trip. Abigail's favourite moment was catching sunrise at Mackinnon Pass and having four kea come to say good morning. The trip was not without it's challenges and Abigail shares the physical and mental obstacles that she overcame.
