Cody and Callie Chase met surfing and adventure in the outdoors has continued to be a constant in their lives, with Callie continuing to surf up to full term pregnancy. With a love for being in nature, Cody and Callie enjoy rock climbing, surfing, rock climbing, skydiving, wind foiling, snowboarding and sailing. Callie shares tips for beginners wanting to learn to surf, including breathwork, strength and learning to read the water. Cody shares his journey to becoming a certified skydiver and about slowing down time when you're in freefall. With a baby on the way, the Chases are eager to share their love for outdoor adventures with their baby girl, and to teach her how to do dangerous things safely. They talk about the way that outdoor pursuits can lead to personal growth and being kinder to yourself, while your problems and worries seem to slip away.
