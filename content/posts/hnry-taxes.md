But I hear you, “why do I have to pay tax?” It’s a legal requirement to pay tax on all of your earnings. There is no tax free threshold. Here’s what IRD says:

> If you're self-employed you use your individual IRD number to pay tax. You pay tax on net profit by filing an individual income return. You can claim back expenses for business activity that you carry out. You need to register for GST if you earn over $60,000 a year.

As noted above, you only pay tax on any “net profit”. This means that if you are spending money towards your business (photography gear, online subscriptions, sub-contractors, etc), you deduct this from your earnings and only pay tax on your profit. Sounds great? That’s where Hnry comes in!
