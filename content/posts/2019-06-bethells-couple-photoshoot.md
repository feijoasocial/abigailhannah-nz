### I always love getting photos taken together with isaac. it’s so special to have these moments to remember and cherish.

![DSCF2791-16.jpg](/images/uploads/2019/06/DSCF2791-16.jpg)

When Karina reached out to me and asked if Isaac & I would be interested in some couple photos, my answer was a firm “yes!” I’m the biggest fan of having our picture taken together and love every opportunity we get to do so.

Coincidentally, the day that we headed out to Bethells Beach for the shoot was the anniversary of being 5 months married.

Karina & Richard were so fun to get to know & spend time with. Karina and I had been following each other on Instagram since forever, so it was great to be able to meet in person. We were super comfortable taking pictures with them & I can’t recommend Karina & Richard enough if you are interested in couple photos or an engagement shoot, don’t hesitate to contact them!
